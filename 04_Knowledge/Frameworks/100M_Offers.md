# 💰 Framework: $100M Offer Structure

## Source
Alex Hormozi - "$100M Offers"

---

## Core Principle

> A Grand Slam Offer is one that is so compelling, people feel stupid saying no.

---

## The Value Equation

```
VALUE = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement)
        ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)
```

**Maximize:** Dream Outcome, Perceived Likelihood
**Minimize:** Time Delay, Effort & Sacrifice

---

## Building the Offer: Checklist

### 1. Define the Dream Outcome
- [ ] What is the end result they deeply desire?
- [ ] How can you articulate it in their words?
- [ ] What's the transformation (Before → After)?

### 2. Identify All Problems
- [ ] What stops them from getting this outcome?
- [ ] What are the next problems AFTER solving the first?
- [ ] What problems arise from the solution itself?

### 3. Solutions List
For each problem, list potential solutions:
- [ ] DIY solutions (low value, high effort)
- [ ] DWY solutions (medium value)
- [ ] DFY solutions (high value, low effort)

### 4. Delivery Mechanisms
- [ ] 1:1 vs 1:Many
- [ ] Live vs Recorded
- [ ] Access level
- [ ] Frequency

### 5. Price Anchoring
- [ ] What's the cost of NOT solving this problem?
- [ ] What would it cost to get this elsewhere?
- [ ] What's the value of the outcome to them?

## Guarantee Types

1. **Unconditional** - Money back, no questions
2. **Conditional** - Money back if X steps completed
3. **Anti-Guarantee** - All sales final (scarcity)
4. **Performance** - Results or refund
5. **Outsized** - 2x money back

---

## Quick Test

Before launching, answer:
- [ ] Would I buy this instantly at this price?
- [ ] Does the value stack make people feel embarrassed at the price?
- [ ] Is there a clear, believable path to the dream outcome?
- [ ] Have I removed as much risk as possible?
