## Email Structure Framework

### SUBJECT LINE
**Goal:** Get the open
**Max Length:** 50 characters (6-10 words)

**Formulas:**
- Question: "Are you making this [X] mistake?"
- Number: "[Number] ways to [outcome]"
- Curiosity: "I can't believe I'm sharing this"
- Direct: "[First Name], quick question"
- Urgency: "[Time limit] to get [benefit]"

## Email Sequence Template (7-Email Launch)

| Day | Email | Focus | Subject Line Idea |
|-----|-------|-------|-------------------|
| 0 | #1 | Announce + Hook | "Something new..." |
| 1 | #2 | The Problem | "Why [X] isn't working" |
| 2 | #3 | The Solution | "The answer to..." |
| 3 | #4 | Social Proof | "What [Name] said about..." |
| 4 | #5 | Objection Handler | "I almost didn't share this" |
| 5 | #6 | Last Chance | "Hours left" |
| 5 | #7 | Final Email | "It's over" |
