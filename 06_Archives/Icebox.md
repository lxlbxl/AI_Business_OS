## Purpose
Storage for ideas that don't align with current strategy but might be valuable later.

---

## Reactivation Criteria

An idea should be moved OUT of the Icebox when:
1. ✅ The North Star changes and this now aligns
2. ✅ Current bottleneck shifts to match this idea
3. ✅ New evidence suggests higher viability
4. ✅ Resources become available

---

## Review Schedule

- **Monthly:** Scan for any quick wins that now align
- **Quarterly:** Full review during strategic planning
