## Source

## Hook Categories

### 1. 🔥 Contrarian / Pattern Interrupt
Challenge what everyone believes.

**Templates:**
- "Everyone says [common belief], but they're wrong. Here's why..."
- "[Popular advice] is keeping you broke/stuck/failed"
- "I used to believe [X]. Then I learned [Y]."
- "Stop doing [common practice]. Do this instead."

**Examples:**
- "Posting daily is killing your content strategy"
- "Your morning routine is making you less productive"

---

### 2. 📊 Curiosity Gap
Create an information gap they NEED to close.

**Templates:**
- "The [adjective] reason [unexpected outcome]"
- "I spent [time] studying [X]. This is what I found."
- "The difference between [A] and [B] is simpler than you think"
- "There are 3 types of [X]. Most people are [worst one]."

**Examples:**
- "I analyzed 1,000 viral posts. 87% had this one thing in common."
- "The $50 difference between broke founders and rich ones"

---

### 3. 🎯 Direct Value Promise
Promise specific, tangible results.

**Templates:**
- "How to [achieve outcome] in [timeframe] (without [pain point])"
- "[Number] ways to [outcome] that work in [year]"
- "The exact [system/process] I used to [result]"
- "Copy my [thing] that generated [impressive result]"

**Examples:**
- "How I grew from 0 to 100K followers in 6 months (no ads)"
- "The 5-minute morning routine that replaced my $300/mo gym"

---

### 4. 😱 High Stakes / Fear
Trigger loss aversion.

**Templates:**
- "If you're doing [X], stop immediately"
- "[X] is dead. Here's what's replacing it."
- "The hidden cost of [common choice]"
- "In [timeframe], [dramatic change]. Are you ready?"

**Examples:**
- "AI is about to make your job obsolete. Unless..."
- "Your biggest competitor just did this. You should too."

---

### 5. 🏆 Proof / Results
Lead with undeniable evidence.

**Templates:**
- "[Timeframe] ago, I was [bad situation]. Now, [impressive result]."
- "I've helped [number] people [achieve outcome]. Here's the pattern."
- "This [thing] made me $[amount] last [timeframe]."
- "[Person/company] used this exact [system] to [result]."

**Examples:**
- "6 months ago, I had $0. This month, I crossed $100K MRR."
- "327 people used this template. Average result: $14K extra revenue."

---

## Hook Formula Builder

```
[EMOTION TRIGGER] + [SPECIFIC DETAIL] + [CURIOSITY GAP]
```

**Worksheet:**
1. What emotion? [Fear/Curiosity/Greed/Pride]
2. What specific? [Number/Time/Reference]
3. What gap? [Incomplete info that demands resolution]

---

## Quick Hook Audit

Before posting, check:
- [ ] First 3 words are punchy
- [ ] Would YOU stop scrolling?
- [ ] No wasted words
- [ ] Can work without context
- [ ] Creates genuine curiosity (not clickbait)
