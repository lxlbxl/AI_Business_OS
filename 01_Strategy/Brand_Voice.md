## Voice Characteristics

### Tone Spectrum

| Dimension | Position | Notes |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| Formal ←→ Casual | [3/5] | Professional but approachable |
| Serious ←→ Playful | [2/5] | Mostly serious, occasional wit |
| Technical ←→ Simple | [3/5] | Clear explanations, no jargon |
| Corporate ←→ Personal | [4/5] | Human, relatable, authentic |

### Voice Pillars

1. **Confident but not arrogant**
   - We know our stuff but we're always learning
   - Lead with value, not with ego

2. **Direct but empathetic**
   - Get to the point
   - Acknowledge the reader's challenges

3. **Strategic but actionable**
   - Big picture thinking
   - But always with next steps

## Writing Rules

### DO:
- ✅ Use "you" and "we" (not "one" or "users")
- ✅ Start with the benefit, not the feature
- ✅ Use active voice
- ✅ Include specific numbers when possible
- ✅ Break complex ideas into bullets

### DON'T:
- ❌ Use buzzwords: "synergy", "leverage" (unless strategic), "pivot"
- ❌ Write walls of text
- ❌ Be vague or wishy-washy
- ❌ Overcomplicate simple concepts
- ❌ Sound like a corporate press release

---

## Content Formats

### Headlines
- Lead with the transformation or outcome
- Use numbers when credible
- Create curiosity without being clickbait

**Formula:** [Outcome] + [Timeframe/Method] + [Without Pain Point]
**Example:** "Build a $1M Business in 12 Months Without Burning Out"

### Hooks
1. Contrarian: Challenge conventional wisdom
2. Story: Start in the middle of action
3. Question: Ask what keeps them up at night
4. Statistic: Lead with surprising data
